# Industry packs: release notes

## Step 7.1: industry packs (first release)

- **Get started → Industry packs:** Clinics, Beauty and wellness, Fitness and studios, and Small business. The one for
  your type of business is recommended.
- **Install** adds WhatsApp templates as drafts (submit them to Meta from Templates), sets up the AI agent's
  instructions and emergency words (keeping yours if you wrote some), creates suggested audiences, and, once Meta
  approves the templates, adds reminder and recall rules switched off. Installing again never changes what you have.
- The onboarding checklist links to the packs once you've chosen your type of business.
