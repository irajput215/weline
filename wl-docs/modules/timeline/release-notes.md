# Contact timeline: release notes

## Step 7.5: contact timeline (first release)

- **Contacts → Contact timeline:** find a contact by name or number and see everything that happened with them, newest
  first: messages, broadcasts, appointments in your connected system, reminders and replies, consent changes, the ad
  or link they came from, what the AI agent did and when they were passed to your team.
- Administrative history only: message contents stay in Chat (one click away), numbers are masked, and looking someone
  up never adds them to your connected system.
