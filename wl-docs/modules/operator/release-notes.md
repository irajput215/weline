# Operator console: release notes

## Step 6.3: the operator console (first release)

- **Weline → Operator console** for Weline super admins: totals, every business with its plan, team and contacts,
  connected system and last message, and for one business its setup progress, this month's usage and a week of
  problems. No patients' details or messages.
- Every look is recorded, and a business's admins see "viewed by Weline staff" in their own audit log.
- Every module now has a test proving another organization can't see or change its data, and a check stops new
  modules from skipping it.
